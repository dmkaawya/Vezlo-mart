How can I place an order?

This is a homepage preview. Message Vezlo Mart on WhatsApp with the products you’re interested in. We’ll confirm the real product, availability and total before you decide to buy.

Which payment methods are available?

Ask us about cash on delivery and bank transfer. Availability depends on the product and delivery option. Always confirm payment details directly with the store.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery fees and estimated arrival times depend on your location, the courier, and the product’s weight. Contact us for a quote for your items.

Can I become a reseller?

Yes, you can enquire about our reseller programme through WhatsApp. Reseller access and pricing require review and approval.
